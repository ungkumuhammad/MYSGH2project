Parity price = blended tariff × ammonia share of LCOE × 5.17 kWh/kg × efficiency × 1,000 kg/t ÷ RM per USD. 5.17 kWh/kg is ammonia LHV of 18.6 MJ/kg ÷ 3.6. Parity means ammonia-fired power at the customer's site costs the same per kWh as the blended tariff above; the remaining share of LCOE is CAPEX and OPEX. The 50% efficiency and 70% share are inputs, not sourced values. USD uses the RM/USD rate entered (4.2, set by the project owner). The price always follows the total blended tariff shown at the top, so it changes with the CRESS share and every tariff input.

Method and assumptions

  • CRESS energy defaults to RM0.48/kWh all-in: RM0.34/kWh solar plus BESS (the balance) and RM0.14/kWh SAC wheeling. Both are editable. Source: project brief.
  • Grid energy uses the High Voltage Non-Domestic Ultra tariff: 55.18 sen/kWh peak, 51.09 sen/kWh off-peak. Peak is 2.00 pm to 10.00 pm Monday to Friday (8 h); off-peak is every other hour. With a flat load, peak share = 8 h × (261 weekdays − 15 selected days) ÷ 8,760 h = 22.5%. Weekdays are 52 × 5 + 1 for 2026, and the 15 selected days are assumed to fall on weekdays.
  • Capacity RM21.76/kW and network RM23.06/kW are per kW of maximum demand per month during the peak period; retail is RM250/month. Source: tariff schedule supplied.
  • Energy = maximum demand × load factor × hours per month. The 100% load factor and 730 h/month (8,760 ÷ 12) are assumptions, not tariff terms. Lower the load factor and the fixed charges weigh more per kWh.
  • Blended tariff = (CRESS energy + grid energy + capacity + network + retail) ÷ total kWh. Taxes, levies and any other surcharge are not included.
