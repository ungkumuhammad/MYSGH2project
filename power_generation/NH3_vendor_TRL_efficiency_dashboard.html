Sources & method

How every TRL badge in Tab 1 was derived, and the full numbered bibliography behind it.

TRL is never a published figure in this table — it is always derived. No OEM, class society, or research institute in any of the 51 sources below states a Technology Readiness Level for its own machine. What is published, per model, is a set of concrete facts — endurance hours, test dates, certification/classification status, whether a customer unit has been sold or installed, whether a vessel or plant is in commercial service. The TRL badge in Tab 1 is the analyst's mapping of those cited facts onto the standard 9-point scale below. Two models with the same badge (e.g. TRL 8) do not mean the same evidence — hover the badge, or find the model's row here, to see exactly which facts drove the call.

TRL scale used

TRLDefinition applied
1–3Basic research / concept — no named hardware, or ideas only
4Component validated in a lab environment
5Component validated in a relevant (representative) environment
6System/subsystem prototype demonstrated in a relevant environment
7System prototype demonstrated in an operational environment
8System complete and qualified (certified/type-approved, or extensively tested as a sellable product) — not yet proven in routine service
9Actual system proven through successful operation, in routine commercial service
