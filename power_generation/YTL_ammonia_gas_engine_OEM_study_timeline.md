## 3. Notes / Assumptions (per CLAUDE.md §7 — flagged, not fabricated)

- **Assumption:** OEM 1:1 discussions can be scheduled within Week 2–3 (1–12
  Sep) — actual availability depends on each OEM's calendar and NDA status;
  confirm per-OEM slot once contacts respond to the kickoff/RFI.
- **Assumption:** "Performance contract" here is scoped as the **framework**
  for guarantee structure (availability, efficiency/heat rate, emissions,
  warranty, LDs) — not a negotiated term sheet with any specific OEM. If YTL
  wants draft commercial term sheets per OEM by 22 Sep, that widens scope and
  should be confirmed explicitly.
- **Assumption:** "Maturity" assessment uses a TRL-style (or CRI-equivalent)
  scale populated from OEM-supplied evidence (test hours, reference
  installations, certification status) — cross-checked where possible against
  the sourced OEM data already in `memory.md` §6 (Wärtsilä 25 Ammonia, MAN
  AmmoniaMot 2, Baker Hughes/Hanwha NovaLT16, IHI IM270, MHI H-25, etc.) so
  OEM self-reported maturity claims aren't taken at face value.
- **Carbon methodology should explicitly include N₂O** (GWP ≈273×CO₂,
  100-yr) and **NH₃ slip**, not just CO₂/NOx — this repo's prior work
  (`power_generation/NH3_gas_turbine_vs_gas_engine_comprehensive_comparison.md`)
  flags N₂O as the ammonia-specific risk that generic combustion-emissions
  templates miss.
- 4 calendar weeks is a **tight** window for OEM-by-OEM technical deep-dives
  across a full shortlist — if the OEM list is long (5+), consider running
  Week 2–3 OEM calls in parallel tracks (multiple MYSGH2/YTL reviewers) rather
  than serially, or flag a scope/timeline trade-off to YTL at kickoff.
