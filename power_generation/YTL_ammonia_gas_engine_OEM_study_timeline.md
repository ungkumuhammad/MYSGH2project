## 3. Notes / Assumptions (per CLAUDE.md §7 — flagged, not fabricated)

- **Decision:** OEM engagement is run entirely by **MISC** — the partner
  company under the parent group that holds the existing OEM relationships —
  not by MYSGH2 or YTL directly. It is modelled as **one continuous
  item/timeline** ("OEM Engagement & Data Collection", 31 Aug–16 Sep)
  covering introductions, data collection, follow-ups and Q&A, rather than
  separate RFI/discussion-round tasks. The dates are MYSGH2's planning
  assumption for when MISC's outreach needs to complete to feed the
  Technology Risk Assessment — confirm actual cadence with MISC.
- **Decision:** Maturity assessment and carbon-emission assessment are
  merged into a single **Technology Risk Assessment** workstream (framework
  build → per-OEM scorecard), rather than run as two separate workstreams.
- **Assumption:** "Performance contract" here is scoped as the **framework**
  for guarantee structure (availability, efficiency/heat rate, emissions,
  warranty, LDs) — not a negotiated term sheet with any specific OEM. If YTL
  wants draft commercial term sheets per OEM by 22 Sep, that widens scope and
  should be confirmed explicitly.
- **Assumption:** The Technology Risk Assessment's maturity component uses a
  TRL-style (or CRI-equivalent) scale populated from OEM-supplied evidence
  (test hours, reference installations, certification status) relayed via
  MISC — cross-checked where possible against the sourced OEM data already in
  `memory.md` §6 (Wärtsilä 25 Ammonia, MAN AmmoniaMot 2, Baker Hughes/Hanwha
  NovaLT16, IHI IM270, MHI H-25, etc.) so OEM self-reported maturity claims
  aren't taken at face value.
- **Its carbon component should explicitly include N₂O** (GWP ≈273×CO₂,
  100-yr) and **NH₃ slip**, not just CO₂/NOx — this repo's prior work
  (`power_generation/NH3_gas_turbine_vs_gas_engine_comprehensive_comparison.md`)
  flags N₂O as the ammonia-specific risk that generic combustion-emissions
  templates miss.
- 4 calendar weeks is a **tight** window for MISC to run OEM engagement
  across a full shortlist in parallel with the Technology Risk Assessment
  build — if the OEM list is long (5+), flag a scope/timeline trade-off to
  YTL and MISC at kickoff.
