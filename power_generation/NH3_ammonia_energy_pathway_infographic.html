Ammonia Energy Pathway — Well to Wake

One kilogram of green ammonia lands at storage carrying its full lower-heating-value energy. Pick an equipment pathway and a cycle configuration below to see how much of that energy actually reaches the wire, and where the rest goes.

Scope: the direct ammonia-firing branch (P2 in memory.md §4) — ship ammonia, fire it as-is. The alternative branch (P1: crack at MMHE → pipe H₂ → H₂-CCGT in Singapore) is a separate pathway, covered in permutations/P1_vs_P2_well-to-wire_energy.md.

Storage detail
  • Refrigerated liquid NH₃, ≈−33 °C at atmospheric pressure (or ≈8.6 bar at ambient).
  • Liquid density ≈680 kg/m³ — derived from 121 kg H₂/m³ ÷ 0.178 wt% H₂ content.
  • Boil-off ≈0.025 %/day — negligible at normal ship-to-genset turnover; only matters at long stream-day storage horizons.

What "usable" means here
  • Net = after the plant's own auxiliary/parasitic load — OEM efficiency figures used above are already stated net.
  • The remainder is rejected as combustor/cylinder heat, exhaust enthalpy, and mechanical/electrical losses — see the waterfall below.
  • Pilot fuel (engine pathway, ~5 % by energy) is an additional external input, not deducted from the NH₃ figures shown here.
