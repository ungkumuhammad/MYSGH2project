Recommendation for the P2 / Singapore-offtake case: the ammonia gas turbine. It is the only ammonia technology with long-duration stationary running, the only one with a credible utility-scale path, the one that solves N₂O in the combustor rather than in a catalyst bed, the one Singapore's own project selected, and the one that gets better if fed cracked ammonia from the MMHE asset. The engine wins a different question — a load-following or incrementally-grown clean block (the 50 MW data-centre framing), where its flat part-load, N+1 granularity, 2-minute start, near-zero water and low tropical derate are decisive.
