Why did we go for Otto concept on Wärtsilä 25 platform?
▪ Wärtsilä have tested both Otto and Diesel concept during the development of Ammonia engine.
▪ Conclusion of those tests were that Wärtsilä selected Otto (low pressure) concept due to:
▪ Safety point-of-view. With low pressure concept you have much lower operating pressures → less complex compared to HP
▪ Performance/efficiency. With Diesel (high pressure) concept you need to have high pressure pump to get the pressure high
enough before injecting to cylinders, that will take approx 3%-unit of the engine efficiency.
▪ Emissions (NOx, N20, NH3): SCR is required for both, low pressure and high-pressure concept to be able to reach IMO Tier 3
level. N2O slip on same level with low pressure concept. NH3 slip after SCR on similar level with both concepts.
▪ Loading performance. During testing & validation phase we have improved loading performance. We see that the loading
performance will be very similar to a LNG DF engine or even better in the future once optimization is done.
▪ Knocking/misfiring. With low pressure ammonia concept we have seen that engine is even less sensitive to misfiring and
knocking compared to LNG DF.

Wärtsilä 25DF Ammonia – safety considerations & machinery protection
Main differences compared to proven DF (LNG)
engine:
1. Gas pipe temperature monitoring added for DF
Ammonia – preventing condensation of gaseous
ammonia in gas pipe
2. Crankcase pressure, same functionality but lower
setpoint for DF Ammonia – suction (negative
pressure) in crankcase for engine room safety & smell
avoidance
3. Pressure monitoring in engine gas manifold
degassing line added for DF Ammonia due to ARMS
Crankcase ventilation system
