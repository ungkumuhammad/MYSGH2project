# Ammonia-Fired Power Generation — OEM Survey ("Who's Who")

> **Scope:** every OEM with a credible, publicly documented ammonia-firing
> programme in (a) gas turbines, (b) two-stroke reciprocating engines,
> (c) four-stroke reciprocating engines. Marine programmes are included and
> labelled as such, because for ammonia the marine engine market is where the
> technology is maturing first — but marine ≠ stationary power, and the table
> says which is which.
> **Basis:** status as publicly announced. No efficiency, cost or emissions
> figure appears here unless the cited source states it. Anything not sourced is
> written as "no known program" or "unconfirmed" — never inferred
> (CLAUDE.md §7, No-Fabrication Rule).
> **Last verified:** **2026-09-10** (web searches run this date).

## 2. Cross-cutting read (evidence-based, no new numbers)

1. **Gas turbines: two credible 100 %-NH₃ tracks, both Japanese-anchored.**
   **IHI** (2 MW IM270, ~3,000 h run, first commercial-plant deployment at
   PETRONAS/Gentari Terengganu from 2027) and **IHI + GE Vernova** (F-class,
   2030). **MHI's H-25 40 MW** is the largest *nameplate* claim but the least
   recently evidenced. Everyone else (Siemens Energy, Ansaldo, Kawasaki,
   Doosan, Solar) is either hydrogen-first or paper-stage on ammonia.
2. **Doosan and Siemens Energy define a third architecture:** don't burn NH₃ —
   **crack it and burn hydrogen**. That is directly relevant to MYSGH2's P1
   (crack at MMHE, pipe H₂) and should not be scored on the same axis as direct
   ammonia firing.
3. **Two-stroke engines are the most commercially advanced ammonia machines of
   any type** — Everllence ME-LGIA (type-approved Jun 2026, ~30+ engines
   ordered across the industry), WinGD X-DF-A (**in service** Jun 2026), J-ENG
   UEC50LSJA (test data published: N₂O ≈3 ppm, >90 % GHG cut). All **marine
   propulsion**; none offered as a stationary genset.
4. **Four-stroke is where stationary ammonia power actually shows up.** The only
   ammonia genset with an announced **commercial sales date** is **IHI Power
   Systems' 6 MW-class 18V28ADF** (sales from JFY2027, explicitly targeting data
   centres and industrial parks). **HD Hyundai** states an intent to serve the
   land power market with HiMSEN; **MAN/Everllence NH3 Spark** targets a
   **pilot-free retrofit** genset; **Bergen AMAZE** targets a retrofit package.
5. **Malaysia is already on the map.** The **IHI–PETRONAS–Gentari** 2 MW
   ammonia-GT demonstration at Terengganu (start 2027) is the first
   ammonia-fired gas turbine deployment announced for a commercial plant
   **in-country** — a direct, near-field precedent for MYSGH2 and a natural
   counterpart to Singapore's EMA/MPA 55–65 MW Jurong Island GT project.

## 3. Methodology & limitations

- **Sources.** OEM press releases and product pages first; then trade press
  (POWER Magazine, Turbomachinery Magazine, Gas Turbine World, Power
  Engineering, Riviera/Maritime Executive/Marine Log/Offshore Energy,
  LNG Prime, S&P Global) and the Ammonia Energy Association. Peer-reviewed
  sources used only where an OEM has published nothing (Ansaldo, Toyota/AIST).
- **Searches run 2026-09-10**; every status statement reflects the most recent
  item found on that date.
- **"No known program" means "no public program located in this survey"**, not
  "does not exist". Absence of evidence is reported as such — see especially
  Kawasaki and Solar Turbines, both large, credible OEMs where a
  non-public/early program is entirely plausible.
- **Blocked domains.** The egress proxy blocked direct fetches of
  `ammoniaenergy.org`, `ihi.co.jp`, `wartsila.com`, `everllence.com` and
  `siemens-energy.com`. Content from those domains was taken from search-engine
  extracts of the same pages, and the canonical URLs are still cited. **Where a
  load-bearing figure depends on one of these (notably the Wärtsilä 25 Ammonia
  delivery year), it is flagged as unresolved rather than asserted.**
- **Marine vs. stationary is not interchangeable.** Type approval for a marine
  genset is not a stationary power-plant product. The table states duty
  explicitly in every engine cell.
