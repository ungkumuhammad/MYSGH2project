# Hydrogen-Fired Power Generation — OEM Survey ("Who's Who")

> **Scope:** OEMs offering, developing, or demonstrating **hydrogen-fired** prime
> movers for power generation: **gas turbines**, **2-stroke gas engines**, and
> **4-stroke gas engines**. Ammonia is *out of scope* here (covered separately in
> `power_generation/NH3_gas_turbine_vs_gas_engine_comprehensive_comparison.md`)
> and is mentioned only where it is the same OEM programme thread.
> **Basis:** hydrogen fractions are quoted **as the source states them** — most
> gas-turbine figures are **vol %**, most engine figures are **vol %** of the fuel
> gas, and some marine-engine figures are **% of fuel energy**. Where the source
> does not state the basis, this is flagged. Nothing is converted or inferred.
> **No-Fabrication Rule (CLAUDE.md §7):** every cell is either sourced to the
> linked item, or explicitly marked *unconfirmed* / *no known programme located*.
> **Last verified: 2026-09-10.**

## 2. Cross-cutting read (derived from the table only)

1. **Gas turbines are furthest ahead on *large* hydrogen.** Every major frame OEM
   (GE Vernova, Siemens Energy, MHI, Ansaldo, Doosan) has a **commercial blend
   rating today** (typically **30–75 vol %**) and a **100 % roadmap landing
   ~2028–2030**. The **only 100 %-H₂ machines that are actually released or
   near-released are small/industrial**: Kawasaki GPB17MMX (1.8 MW, dry), Baker
   Hughes NovaLT16 (~16 MW), GE Vernova B/E-class DLN retrofit (from 2026),
   GE Vernova LM6000VELOX (Whyalla, commissioning early 2026).
2. **The 4-stroke engine side has genuinely caught up at MW scale.** Wärtsilä's
   **31H2** running on **100 % H₂ into the Spanish grid (Jun 2026)** at
   **4.6–10.4 MW/unit** is the single most decision-relevant 2026 datapoint for a
   modular hydrogen power block — it has no ammonia equivalent (see repo
   `NH3_gas_turbine_vs_gas_engine_comprehensive_comparison.md`, where the ammonia
   engine is still marine-first).
3. **The 2-stroke column is nearly empty, and is marine-only where it exists.**
   Only **J-ENG (6UEC35LSGH, >95 % H₂ energy at full load, Mar 2026)** and the
   **MAN B&W ME-GI single-cylinder conversion by MITSUI (2024)** exist, both as
   demonstrations aimed at **shipping in the 2030s** — **no stationary
   power-generation 2-stroke hydrogen product exists from any OEM.**
4. **Distinguish dry (DLN/DLE) from diffusion firing.** Several "100 % hydrogen"
   claims are **diffusion-flame** results (e.g. Kawasaki Kobe 2020), which
   normally carry a NOx/diluent penalty; the commercially meaningful milestones
   are the **dry** ones (Kawasaki GPB17MMX 2023; GE Vernova B/E-class DLN <25 ppm
   NOx, Jan 2025). Do not merge the two categories.
5. **Retrofit is a distinct competitive lane.** Hanwha/PSM **FlameSheet** and
   GE Vernova's B/E-class DLN are aimed at the **installed fleet**, not new units
   — relevant if any MYSGH2 permutation contemplates converting an existing
   Singapore/Johor gas turbine rather than buying new.
